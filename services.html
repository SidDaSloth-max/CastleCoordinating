Wedding Planning & Event Coordination, Done Right

Planning a wedding or a large event takes more than a checklist — it takes someone who can hold the entire picture in their head while still tracking the smallest details. As a wedding planner and event coordinator, I work with couples and hosts across Washington State to turn a vision into a real, working plan: a venue that fits the day you're imagining, vendors who show up and deliver, a timeline that actually holds together, and a design that feels like you. Whether you're searching for a "wedding planner near me" for full-service planning or just need an experienced day-of coordinator to run the show, Castle Coordinating & Event Planning is built around one goal — a stress-free process for you and a spectacular result on the day itself.

Every couple's needs are different, which is why services are never one-size-fits-all. Some couples want full-service wedding planning from the very first vendor meeting; others have planned most of it themselves and just need a day-of coordinator to take the reins so they can relax. Corporate hosts often need a completely different kind of event planning — tighter logistics, brand consistency, and a polished guest experience for a gala or business event. Whatever the scale, I bring the same precision, organization, and genuine care to every wedding and event I plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a wedding planner?

Many couples reach out 9-12 months before their wedding date, especially for full-service planning, but I also take on day-of coordination and shorter-timeline events. It's always worth reaching out even if your date is close.

What's the difference between a wedding planner and a day-of coordinator?

A wedding planner is involved from early planning through the event, helping with vendors, budget, design, and logistics. A day-of coordinator steps in during the final weeks to confirm details and run the wedding day itself, so a couple who has already done their own planning can still be fully present.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes — destination wedding planning is one of my specialties, whether that means a coastal getaway, a mountain venue, or a celebration further from home.

Do you plan events other than weddings?

Yes. I also plan corporate galas, private celebrations, showers, and other social events, bringing the same precision and passion to every occasion.
