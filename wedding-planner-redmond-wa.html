Wedding & Event Planning in Redmond, WA

Redmond has grown into one of the Eastside's most dynamic and fast-changing cities, and that shows in the weddings I plan here — from big, joyful celebrations that reflect the international community drawn by the area's tech industry, to smaller, quieter ceremonies tucked into a park pavilion or a family's own backyard, each with its own planning needs. Marymoor Park is a frequent request, with its open lawns, picnic shelters, and easy access for guests, but I also plan weddings on private estates along Bear Creek and the Sammamish River, and in the neighborhoods around Downtown Redmond and Redmond Town Center.

Redmond weddings often come with a few things in common: guest lists that span multiple countries and traditions, a practical need to work around weekday commute and traffic patterns for local guests, and venues that range from formal event spaces to a family's own backyard. I handle the planning either way — coordinating vendors, building a realistic day-of timeline, and making sure any cultural or religious traditions you want included are planned for properly from the start rather than squeezed in as an afterthought once the rest of the day is already set. If you're searching for a "wedding planner near me" in Redmond, I'd love to hear about your vision and help you shape it into a workable plan.

Redmond is also a growing city, with ongoing development around downtown and the light rail extension changing traffic patterns from year to year. I keep an eye on that when I'm planning guest logistics — road closures, construction detours, and parking availability near a venue can shift, so I confirm current conditions close to your date rather than relying on old information when I'm building out arrival instructions for your guests.

What It's Like Working With Me on a Redmond Wedding

Because Redmond weddings often involve blending traditions or coordinating with family members traveling from out of the country, I put extra care into the planning conversations up front — making sure I understand what matters most to you and to your families before I start building a timeline. From there, you're working with one person throughout: the same planner handling your first venue walkthrough is the one running your ceremony rehearsal and your wedding day itself, with no account manager or hand-off in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan weddings at Marymoor Park?

Yes. Marymoor Park's picnic shelters and open lawns are a popular choice for Redmond weddings, and I coordinate rental logistics, vendor load-in, and timing around the park's other scheduled activities.

Can you plan a wedding with a diverse or multicultural guest list?

Yes. Redmond's international community means I regularly plan weddings that blend multiple traditions, and I work closely with you to make sure every cultural element of your ceremony and reception is represented the way you want.

Do you help with corporate events near the Redmond tech campuses?

Yes. Alongside weddings, I plan corporate galas and private events for companies and teams based around Redmond, handling everything from venue selection to full-day logistics.

Can I hire you just for day-of coordination if I've planned everything else?

Yes. Many Redmond couples plan the bulk of their wedding themselves and bring me in closer to the date to confirm vendors, build the final timeline, and run the day itself so family members aren't stuck managing logistics.
