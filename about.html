About Castle Coordinating & Event Planning

A personal, founder-led approach to wedding and event planning — 5-star rated and available 24 hours.

My Approach to Wedding & Event Planning

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, Castle Coordinating & Event Planning crafts seamless and unforgettable events. Specializing in bespoke wedding and event planning, my dedication transforms your vision into a stunning reality.

I handle every detail with precision and passion, ensuring a stress-free and spectacular experience. Whether you're dreaming of a destination wedding or a lavish corporate gala, I am at your service.

Founder-Led, From Start to Finish

Castle Coordinating & Event Planning was established in 2023 on a simple idea: every couple and host deserves a planner who is genuinely invested in their day, not just managing a checklist. When you work with Castle Coordinating, you work directly with me — from our very first conversation through the last moment of your event. There's no hand-off to a junior coordinator and no account manager relaying your requests; the person planning your wedding or event is the same person you'll be talking to the whole way through.

What You Can Expect

  • Personal, one-on-one planning from your first inquiry to your event day
  • Precise attention to vendors, timelines, logistics, and design details
  • Availability around the clock — questions and last-minute details don't wait for business hours
  • Experience with weddings, destination celebrations, corporate galas, and private events
  • A 5-star rating on Google from couples and hosts I've worked with

Serving Washington State & Beyond

Castle Coordinating & Event Planning is based in Washington State and plans weddings and events throughout the region, along with destination celebrations wherever your vision takes us. Whatever the scale of your event — an intimate elopement or a lavish corporate gala — I bring the same precision, care, and passion to every detail.
