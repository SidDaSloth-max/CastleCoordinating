Wedding & Event Planning in Everett, WA

As the Snohomish County seat, Everett brings together a working waterfront on Port Gardner Bay, a historic downtown with real character, and easy access to the rest of the county — which makes it a great home base for a wedding that pulls guests from both Seattle and the county's smaller towns further north and east. I plan ceremonies along the waterfront near the marina, receptions in Everett's historic downtown event spaces, and everything the logistics of an Everett wedding require, from parking for a downtown venue to weather planning for an outdoor water-view ceremony.

Everett's mix of waterfront and historic charm means couples here often want a day that feels a little different from a typical Eastside wedding — something with more genuine character and less corporate polish, without losing any of the careful coordination that makes a wedding day actually run smoothly behind the scenes. I handle that balance: keeping a historic venue's quirks (older buildings, limited load-in access, unique layouts) from becoming a problem on the day, while still delivering the same level of vendor coordination and timeline planning I bring to any wedding. If you're searching for a "wedding planner near me" in Everett, I'd love to talk through your venue.

Everett's downtown has also been steadily revitalizing over the past several years, with new restaurants and event spaces opening alongside the historic buildings that have always been there. I keep track of what's opened recently and what each space is actually suited for, so if you're not set on a specific venue yet, I can point you toward options that fit your guest count and style rather than just the most obvious choice.

What It's Like Working With Me on an Everett Wedding

Waterfront weather and older buildings both call for a planner who thinks through contingencies rather than hoping for the best. I build a weather backup into any outdoor Everett ceremony from the start, and I walk historic venues in advance so I know exactly where vendors will load in, where there might be access issues, and how to keep your day running smoothly even in a building that wasn't originally built for events. You're working with the same person for all of it, start to finish — from the first site visit to the last vendor payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan waterfront weddings in Everett?

Yes. Everett's Port Gardner Bay waterfront, including the marina and nearby parks, is a setting I plan around regularly, coordinating weather contingencies and the logistics of an outdoor water-view ceremony.

Can you plan a wedding at a historic Everett venue?

Yes. Everett has a number of historic buildings and event spaces in its downtown core, and I'm glad to coordinate a wedding or event in a venue with that kind of character.

Do you get a marriage license for us in Snohomish County?

Obtaining your marriage license is something you'll handle directly with Snohomish County, since it requires both parties in person, but I'm happy to make sure it's on your planning timeline so it doesn't get missed.

Do you have a backup plan for waterfront weather?

Yes. Any outdoor Everett ceremony I plan includes a realistic weather contingency from the start, so a forecast change the week of your wedding doesn't turn into a scramble to find an indoor option.
