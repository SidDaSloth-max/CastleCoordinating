Privacy Policy

How Castle Coordinating & Event Planning handles your information. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who I am

Castle Coordinating & Event Planning is a wedding and event planning business serving Washington State. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Castle Coordinating & Event Planning. You can reach me at castlecoordinating@gmail.com or (360) 381-4022.

What I collect

Information you send me. If you use the quote, contact, or feedback form on this site, I receive what you type into it: your name, email address, phone number (optional), event date, the type of event you are planning, approximate guest count, and your message. If you email or call me, I also receive whatever you choose to share.

Information collected automatically. This site uses Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages and buttons are used (for example, which pages are viewed and when the call, email, or review buttons are clicked). Google Analytics may collect your approximate location, device and browser type, and an identifier stored in a cookie. I do not receive your name or email from Google Analytics.

Fonts. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers (which can see your IP address) when a page loads.

How I use it

  • To reply to your questions and prepare quotes.
  • To plan and coordinate your wedding or event if you hire me.
  • To understand how the site is used and make it better.
  • To keep the site and its forms safe from spam and abuse.

I do not sell your personal information.

Who else handles it

  • Netlify hosts this site and receives and stores form submissions before they are emailed to me.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and Google Fonts, and Gmail is used for email.

These companies process information on my behalf and under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technology. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics. The site still works if you do.

How long I keep it

I keep inquiries and event details for as long as I need them to respond to you, plan your event, and keep ordinary business records. You can ask me to delete your information at any time.

Your requests

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email castlecoordinating@gmail.com and I will respond as soon as I can.

Photos on this site

The album and gallery show events I have planned, with credit to the photographers. If you appear in a photo here and would like it removed, email me and I will take it down.

Children

This site is meant for adults planning events. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Links to other sites

This site links to Facebook, Instagram, The Knot, and Google. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for how they handle your information.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions about your information? Just ask.
