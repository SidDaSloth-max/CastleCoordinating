Wedding & Event Planning Along the Kirkland Waterfront

Kirkland's Lake Washington shoreline is one of the prettiest backdrops on the Eastside, and it shows in how many couples ask for a waterfront wedding here. As a wedding planner working in Kirkland, I coordinate ceremonies at the lakefront parks near Marina Park and the Moss Bay neighborhood, receptions at the restaurants and event spaces lining the waterfront, and everything in between — vendor load-in near a busy downtown, guest parking during a summer weekend when the boardwalk is full of visitors, and timelines built around the light over the lake for golden-hour photos, which shifts throughout the wedding season and is worth planning your ceremony time around.

Downtown Kirkland brings its own planning considerations: narrow streets, limited parking, and a lively art-walk and restaurant scene that means popular venues book quickly, especially for Friday and Saturday evenings in summer. I handle the logistics — coordinating with venues on load-in windows, managing vendor timing so your florist and caterer aren't competing for the same loading zone, and building a day-of schedule that accounts for Kirkland's foot traffic and boat activity on the lake. If you're picturing a "wedding planner near me" who knows the Eastside waterfront well, that's exactly the kind of planning I do.

Summer is peak season for a Kirkland waterfront wedding, which also means it overlaps with the busiest boating and tourist traffic of the year on the lake. I build that into the planning conversation early — from realistic guest arrival windows on a summer Saturday to backup timing if a ceremony needs to shift around sunset for the best light. If your date lands near a major summer weekend, I also flag that early so we're not scrambling to adjust plans once vendors and venues are already locked in.

What It's Like Working With Me on a Kirkland Wedding

I keep a close relationship with each Kirkland venue I work in rather than treating waterfront parks and downtown event spaces as interchangeable. Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan waterfront weddings in Kirkland?

Yes. Kirkland's Lake Washington waterfront is one of the most requested settings I work with, and I coordinate around park permits, marina logistics, and the timing that comes with an outdoor lakeside ceremony.

Can you plan a restaurant or rooftop reception in downtown Kirkland?

Yes. Downtown Kirkland has a number of waterfront restaurants and event spaces that work well for smaller receptions, and I handle vendor coordination and timeline planning for those venues just as I would a larger one.

Do you help with permits for a park wedding in Kirkland?

I help you understand what your chosen park or venue requires and keep that process on track alongside the rest of your planning, though permit applications themselves are typically submitted by the couple or venue.

Can you coordinate guest parking for a downtown Kirkland venue?

Yes. Limited downtown parking is one of the more common concerns I hear from Kirkland couples, and I plan around it — whether that means confirming valet with your venue or giving guests clear guidance ahead of time.
