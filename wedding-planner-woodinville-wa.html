Wedding & Event Planning for Woodinville Wine Country

Woodinville is one of the most requested destinations I work in, and it's easy to see why: with more than a hundred wineries and tasting rooms packed into a few square miles, it's become one of the Pacific Northwest's most popular settings for a wedding. As a wedding planner working throughout Woodinville, I help couples navigate everything that comes with a winery wedding — venue-specific rules on outside vendors, tasting room layouts that double as reception space, and timelines built around both a ceremony and a full evening of hosting. Whether your venue sits along the Sammamish River Valley or up in the hills above the Hollywood District, I coordinate the details so the day feels effortless.

What makes Woodinville weddings unique is the built-in destination feel — many guests turn the weekend into a wine-tasting trip of their own, which means logistics like parking, shuttles between tasting rooms, and lodging recommendations often become part of the planning conversation. I handle that alongside the traditional wedding-day coordination: vendor scheduling, ceremony and reception timelines, and making sure your venue's pour schedule and your wedding timeline don't collide. If you're searching for a "wedding planner near me" in Woodinville, I'd love to talk through your vision and your venue.

Timing matters more in Woodinville than in a lot of places I work. Late summer and early fall are the most popular wedding months here, which is also harvest season for the wineries — some venues get busier with their own events during that stretch, so booking early and confirming exclusivity with your venue is worth doing well ahead of time. I keep an eye on that overlap when I'm helping a couple choose a date, so you're not competing with a crush event for your venue's attention on your own wedding weekend.

What It's Like Working With Me on a Woodinville Wedding

Because I take on a limited number of weddings at a time, I'm able to get to know your specific venue rather than treating every winery the same way. That means walking the space with you if I can, understanding where your caterer will set up, how guests will move between the ceremony spot and the reception area, and what your venue's own event coordinator needs from me versus what falls on your outside vendors. You get one point of contact for all of it — not a rotating account manager, but the same person from our first conversation through your last dance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan weddings at Woodinville wineries?

Yes. Winery and vineyard weddings are one of the most common requests I get from Woodinville couples, and I'm happy to coordinate with your venue's own event team to make sure every detail — from the tasting room layout to your ceremony timeline — comes together smoothly.

Can you help with hotel blocks for out-of-town wedding guests?

Yes. A lot of Woodinville weddings draw guests who are also visiting for the wine tasting, so I help coordinate lodging recommendations and logistics alongside the wedding day itself.

How far in advance should I book a Woodinville wedding planner?

Woodinville's most popular winery venues book up well in advance, often a year or more out for peak summer and fall dates, so I recommend reaching out as soon as you have a venue in mind — or before, if you'd like help choosing one.

I've already booked my winery — do you still offer day-of coordination?

Yes. Plenty of Woodinville couples plan most of the details themselves and just want someone experienced running the actual wedding day, confirming vendor arrivals, and keeping the timeline on track. That's exactly what day-of coordination is for.
