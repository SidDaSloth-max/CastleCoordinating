Wedding & Event Planning in Bellevue, WA

As the Eastside's largest and most established city, Bellevue offers a wider range of wedding settings than almost anywhere else I plan in — the Bellevue Botanical Garden's forested trails and display gardens for a soft, natural ceremony; downtown high-rise hotels and event venues for something more formal, with skyline and Lake Washington views; and waterfront parks like Meydenbauer Bay for a mix of natural scenery and city convenience. I coordinate weddings and events across all of it, matching the planning approach to what each venue actually requires, whether that means a 300-guest black-tie reception downtown or a small, quiet garden ceremony for close family only.

A downtown Bellevue wedding usually means coordinating with an in-house hotel or venue event team, navigating valet and guest parking in a busy urban core, and building a timeline around load-in windows shared with other events in the same building. A garden or waterfront ceremony brings a different set of details — weather contingencies, permit requirements, and moving guests and vendors between an outdoor ceremony site and an indoor reception space. Whichever direction your Bellevue wedding takes, I handle the logistics so you're not the one chasing down vendors or double-checking permit paperwork the week of your event.

Downtown Bellevue also stays busy year-round with its own calendar of festivals, holiday events, and conventions, which can affect hotel availability and event-space calendars around popular dates. I factor that into planning early, especially for couples hoping to book a downtown hotel venue, so we're working around the city's broader event calendar from the start instead of discovering a scheduling conflict after contracts are already signed and deposits are down.

What It's Like Working With Me on a Bellevue Wedding

Bellevue weddings often involve more moving pieces than a smaller-venue wedding — multiple vendor teams, a formal venue with its own house rules, and sometimes a guest list that spans both casual and black-tie expectations. I bring the same close, hands-on approach regardless of scale: one dedicated planner managing every detail, from your first venue tour through the final vendor payment, so nothing falls into the gap between your venue's in-house team and your outside vendors, and no detail gets lost or forgotten between the two.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan weddings at the Bellevue Botanical Garden?

Yes. The Bellevue Botanical Garden is a beautiful and popular ceremony setting, and I coordinate around its permit process, garden layout, and the timing needed to move guests between ceremony and reception locations.

Can you plan a black-tie wedding or gala in downtown Bellevue?

Yes. Bellevue's downtown hotels and event venues are well suited to larger, more formal celebrations, and full-scale black-tie weddings and corporate galas are very much within what I plan.

Do you coordinate with Bellevue hotel event teams?

Yes. Working alongside a hotel or venue's in-house event team is a normal part of planning a Bellevue wedding, and I make sure your vision, their venue logistics, and your outside vendors are all coordinated instead of working in separate directions.

Can you recommend vendors if I don't already have a florist or caterer?

Yes. I'm happy to recommend vendors I trust for your Bellevue wedding, though I'm equally comfortable working with a vendor team you've already chosen — the coordination approach is the same either way.
